What are we filling in for you?+

The model uses 75 property inputs. We start from a representative training home of the same type in your chosen neighborhood, then replace its main details with your answers. If that combination has no training homes, we use a representative home of that type elsewhere in Ames.

Basement, lot, half bathrooms, renovation year, maintenance condition, and other unasked details come from that example. Garage area is assumed at 240 sq ft per car. These choices affect the estimate. Market change is applied automatically while holding these characteristics fixed.

The five finish levels map to the model’s material-quality scores of 2, 4, 5, 7, and 9 out of 10. Maintenance condition is a separate, automatically filled field. The estimate download includes all supplied and assumed model inputs, the latest available index period, and the market-index source.

 BEHIND THE NUMBER

A model with context.

The histogram gradient boosting model learns from 2,589 Ames sales from 2006–2009. Every training price is first adjusted to a common market reference using the FHFA index. The model learns property values at that level, then the latest published market adjustment is applied automatically. Sale year and month are excluded from its 75 property inputs.

Automatic market adjustment

Accuracy for the adjusted period is unknown. There are no recent property-level sale records in this project. A retrospective 2010 check uses revised index values and actual sale-quarter indices, so it is not an as-of-date forecast. An average historical error is not an individual prediction interval.

Where it applies

Ames houses and townhouses built by 2010, with the supplied property characteristics held fixed. Renovations, aging, and neighborhood-specific price changes are not predicted. Individual apartments, newer buildings, and other cities remain unsupported.
