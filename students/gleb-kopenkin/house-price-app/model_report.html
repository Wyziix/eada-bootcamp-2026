What each KPI means

MAE
Mean absolute error. On average, how many dollars the prediction is off. The easiest one to explain to anyone.
RMSE
Root mean squared error. Like MAE but big misses count extra. If RMSE is much bigger than MAE, the model makes some large mistakes.
R²
Share of the price differences the model explains. 1.0 is perfect, 0 means no better than always guessing the average price.
MAPE
Mean absolute percentage error. Average error as a % of the real price, so a $20k miss on a $100k house counts more than on a $400k one.
Within 10%
Share of houses where the prediction lands within 10% of the real price. A practical "how often is it close enough" number.
CV MAE
MAE averaged over 5 different train/validation splits of the training data. The ± tells you how much the score moves depending on which houses it trains on.
Train
Seconds to fit the model on the training houses, on a 4-core machine.
Size
How big the saved model file is. Matters if you put it in an app or a web page.
