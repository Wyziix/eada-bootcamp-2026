What drives price

Correlation with price from the EDA (eda.py). +1 means they rise together, −1 means one falls as the other rises.

Income dominates. Location matters too but shows up weakly here because it isn't a straight line: both the far north and the far south are cheaper than the coast around LA and San Francisco. Trees catch that pattern, which is why gradient boosting beats linear regression in train.py.
